OUR CORE SERVICES

INTERNATIONAL TRADING

Sourcing and procurement of a wide range of industrial, commercial, and consumer goods with a strong supplier network across Iran.

PAYMENT FACILITATION

Due to SWIFT unavailability, we offer alternative and compliant payment structures through:

  • Third-country financial intermediaries
  • Trade settlement via barter, crypto (case-by-case), or trusted agent arrangements
  • Coordination with regional banking and non-banking channels

CROSS-STUFFING & TRANSSHIPMENT

Operating through logistics hubs in UAE, Oman, and neighboring countries, goods are cross-stuffed and rerouted using efficient and secure methods. Seamless coordination between ports ensures cargo continuity despite direct shipping restrictions.

SANCTIONS-AWARE TRADE SOLUTIONS

Tailor-made logistics, financial, and compliance strategies to meet the needs of customers operating under restrictive environments. Ensuring documentation, packaging, and routing align with global trade compliance standards.

CONSULTATION SERVICES FOR BUSINESS PARTNERS

We provide comprehensive consultation services to all our business partners, ensuring compliance with the complex and evolving trade rules of Iran. We offer tailored guidance that includes:

  • Strict adherence to Iran's trade regulations and international sanctions solutions.
  • Management of multiple and fluctuating exchange rates to optimize outcomes.
  • Understanding of legal implications to mitigate risks and ensure smooth operations.
